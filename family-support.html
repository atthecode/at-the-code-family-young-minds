WHY THIS EXISTS

Sometimes the barrier isn't willingness. It's the cost of getting there.

Travel

Train, bus and approved licensed taxi/Uber costs for contact, social-work meetings, assessments and other important appointments.

Food

Food or restricted vouchers for parents and children during long travel, contact and appointment days.

A safe bed

Approved hotel accommodation where distance or circumstances make same-day travel unrealistic.

Family contact

Safe handovers, approved venues, one appropriate activity and practical items needed for meaningful family time.

Paid facilitators

Facilitators are paid for their work, preparation, notes and reasonable work-related travel expenses.

Other barriers

Approved essentials directly connected to maintaining family relationships or participating in children’s-services arrangements.

FAMILY APPEALS

Families can tell us the problem. We can ask the community to help solve it.

Approved family stories can be published anonymously. We remove identifying information before anything goes public. Families do not automatically receive cash.

EXAMPLE OF HOW AN APPEAL WILL LOOK

Family 001 · South East

A parent needs help with return train travel, food during a long contact day and local transport to the agreed contact venue.

Needed£94

When an approved need is funded, AT THE CODE will normally buy the ticket, book the hotel/service or provide an appropriate restricted voucher. Direct cash is reserved for exceptional approved circumstances.

ASK FOR SUPPORT

Tell us what's getting in the way.

Your first enquiry should be brief. Do not send children's full names, addresses, school names, case numbers or full court/social-work documents.

Support is subject to checks, available community funding and any relevant contact arrangements. We will not knowingly fund something that conflicts with a court order, injunction, local-authority plan or safeguarding restriction.

THE LONG-TERM VISION

The first AT THE CODE Family House.

After proving the support network, the aim is to purchase and renovate a permanent property where families can spend safe, ordinary, meaningful time together.

Family contact roomsSafe handover spaceKitchen & cookingArt roomMusic / recording studioQuiet / sensory spaceGardenMeeting rooms

The property may include a completely separate private residential area for the founder's family, helping reduce ongoing operating costs. This will be disclosed to property-fund donors and kept separate from public family-support areas.

Contribute to Family House

Safety, dignity and privacy come first.

Public family appeals are moderated. Identifying details are removed. A family's story is not automatically published just because they apply. Appropriate vetting, references and safeguarding processes will be used for facilitators, and a DBS certificate alone is not treated as proof of suitability.
