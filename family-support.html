WHAT THE COMMUNITY CAN COVER

Practical help, exactly where it is needed.

Families do not automatically receive cash. AT THE CODE normally buys the ticket, books the approved service, pays the facilitator or provides an appropriate restricted voucher.

↔

Travel to contact

Train, bus, approved taxi or Uber costs for contact, assessments, social-work meetings and other important appointments.

♥

Food & essentials

Food during long travel or contact days, plus restricted vouchers and agreed essentials where appropriate.

⌂

Safe accommodation

Approved hotel support where distance or circumstances make same-day travel unrealistic.

◎

Safe handovers

Structured pickup, drop-off and handover support where parents should not or cannot meet directly.

★

One family activity

An approved main activity such as art, music, cinema, bowling, cooking or another suitable local option.

✓

Paid facilitators

Facilitators are paid for session work, preparation, notes and reasonable work-related travel expenses.

ASK FOR SUPPORT

Tell us what is getting in the way.

Keep your first enquiry short. Do not include children’s full names, home addresses, school names, case numbers or full court/social-work documents.

ImportantSupport is subject to checks, available funding and any relevant court, local-authority or safeguarding arrangements.
