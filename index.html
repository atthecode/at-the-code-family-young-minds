Family learning, built differently

Helping children grow through learning, play and everyday life.

AT THE CODE – Family & Young Minds is creating thoughtful digital experiences that help children learn, build confidence, develop positive behaviour and enjoy meaningful activities with the people who care for them.

“Technology should help families feel more capable — not more overwhelmed.”

Our approach is simple: useful first, safe by design, enjoyable enough to come back to.

It started with a real family need.

The idea grew from lived experience: difficult periods, pressure at home, lockdown, and the challenge of finding safe, useful and genuinely engaging things for children to do. Rather than accept that gap, we started imagining what a better experience could look like.

More than screen time.

Children need more than something to keep them occupied. They need experiences that help them understand the world, practise positive choices, build practical skills and feel proud of what they can do. Parents and carers also need tools that feel supportive, not complicated.

That is the space AT THE CODE – Family & Young Minds is being built to fill.

Technology with a human purpose.

We use technology where it genuinely improves the experience: personalising learning, making activities interactive and helping children stay engaged. The technology is not the headline. The child, the family and the outcome are.

What we’re building.

One connected family-focused venture, with each part designed to solve a different everyday need. The public sees the direction; the deeper roadmap stays private while we build.

01 / LEARNING

Orish

A child-safe learning guide that can explain, encourage and turn lessons into playful challenges. Orish is being designed around academic learning, behaviour, emotional understanding and life skills.

Learn through play, not pressure.
02 / PRACTICAL LIFE

Butter Cakes & Bakes

Family cooking and practical learning through measuring, following steps, taking turns and making something together. Everyday activities become opportunities to build confidence and independence.

Real skills, shared at home.
03 / FAMILY EXPERIENCE

Family Cinema

A future digital experience with clearly separated spaces for children, families and adults. The children’s side is designed around appropriate content, learning and safe creative experiences.

Clear boundaries. Better experiences.

What matters to us.

We do not want to make “another AI platform.” We are building a family product with clear values, and technology sits quietly underneath the experience where it is useful.

Children come before features.

Safety, age-appropriateness and healthy boundaries shape the product from the beginning.

Learning should feel alive.

Games, stories, practical activities and curiosity make learning easier to return to.

Behaviour is learned in context.

Communication, patience, honesty and respect are built naturally into situations and choices.

Parents remain in control.

Adults should understand what children are using and have clear control over access and settings.

Technology should support people.

We use it to make learning more useful and accessible, not to replace human relationships.

Build carefully, then grow.

Start with strong experiences, learn from real families and expand only where it adds real value.

The road ahead.

The goal is not to release everything at once. It is to build a strong first experience, prove what works and expand responsibly from there.

Now

Build the core.

Bring Orish to life, create the first polished learning games, establish parent controls and test the experience.

Next

Learn from families.

Use real feedback to improve the learning, behaviour and family-activity experience before wider growth.

Later

Grow the world.

Add new learning experiences and connected family spaces without losing the principles that made the first version work.

A public window, not the full blueprint.

This site shares the purpose, direction and values behind AT THE CODE – Family & Young Minds. Families, supporters, collaborators and funders can understand what we are building without us publishing every idea before it is ready.
